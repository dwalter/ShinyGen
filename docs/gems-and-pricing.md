# Gems and pricing

> Canonical version: **<https://shinygen.ai/docs/credits>** · Prices: **<https://shinygen.ai/pricing>**

Shiny Gen is an AI game maker. It is free to make, play, and share games, and no
subscription is required. AI generation uses gems, and new accounts get 30 free gems. When you want more,
buy a $2.99 starter pack, a gem pack that never expires, or an optional membership. Exporting your games as files requires a purchase.

## What's free

- Making, playing, sharing, and remixing games, and multiplayer.
- Building and editing games by hand, including editing their code and assets yourself, and importing
  your own images.
- Code written by an AI assistant you connect over MCP, such as Claude or ChatGPT (it runs on your own
  AI plan).
- 30 free gems for new accounts, and one free AI agent prompt each day once your gems run out.

## What a purchase unlocks

Exporting your games as files requires a purchase. It covers a zip for
the web you can host anywhere (it makes no calls to Shiny Gen and plays offline once loaded), a ROM for
GBA, GBC, and N64 games, a .dol for GameCube games, and a .nds for DS games. Any purchase also lifts the one-time free allowance of actions (currently 100) on a connected AI assistant. See
[Export your game as a ROM](https://shinygen.ai/export-rom).

## What uses gems

Gems are Shiny Gen's credit currency. They pay for AI generation: game code from the built-in AI agent,
images, sprite animations, 3D models, sound effects, and music. The built-in agent spends gems for each
chat step based on the model you pick. Each generation shows its gem cost before it runs, the agent's
model picker shows what each model costs, and your balance is always visible in the app.

## AI assistants

An outside assistant you [connect over MCP](mcp.md) runs on your own AI plan, so its code edits cost no
gems; only the AI generation it runs spends your gems. Shiny Gen's built-in AI agent runs its model on
Shiny Gen, so each of its chat steps spends gems as well.

Two limits apply to a connected assistant:

- **A daily gem cap** limits how many gems an assistant can spend in one day. It applies to the built-in
  agent too.
- **A one-time free allowance of actions.** Accounts that have never made a purchase get a one-time
  allowance of assistant actions that change something (writing code, generating, running the game),
  currently 100. It does not reset. Reading is never limited, and any purchase, from the $2.99 starter
  pack, removes the allowance for good. Once you have connected an outside assistant, the built-in
  agent's generations count toward the same allowance.

See [Connect Claude or ChatGPT](mcp.md) for the details.
