# Support

> Canonical version: **<https://shinygen.ai/support>**

## Contact us

We aim to respond to all inquiries within 24 hours during business days.

- **Email:** support@shinygen.ai
- **Business hours:** Monday–Friday, 9 AM – 5 PM EST

## Technical requirements

- A modern web browser (Chrome, Safari, or Firefox — Chrome is recommended)
- A stable internet connection
- WebGPU or WebGL2 — Shiny Gen renders with WebGPU where it is available and falls back to WebGL2 automatically, so effectively any modern browser works
- Minimum 4 GB RAM recommended

## How do I report a bug?

Email us with **"BUG REPORT"** in the subject line and include:

- Your browser and version
- Steps to reproduce the issue
- What you expected to happen
- What actually happened

## What's your refund policy?

Gem purchases are final and non-refundable, except where required by applicable law. Gems are spent on
AI generation, which costs real compute the moment you run it, so they cannot be returned once a
generation has run. Gems bought in a one-time pack never expire.

If you have a membership, you can cancel it at any time: you will not be charged again, and you keep
your membership and any remaining gems until the end of the current billing period. Cancelling does not
refund or prorate the period you are already in. See the
[Payment Terms](https://shinygen.ai/payment-terms) for the full terms.

## Can I transfer my account?

Currently, account transfers are not supported.
